Most task apps mix today's work with ideas that can wait. Sometime separates tasks by when they matter. Use Today, Soon, and Sometime without projects, tags, or a complex planning system.

* Write a task and use natural dates and times when you need them.
* Add local reminders and recurring schedules.
* Pin important tasks as Android notifications.
* See current tasks in Android home screen widgets.
* Use Spaces to separate parts of your life or work.
* Choose light, dark, soft, Material You, or custom colors.
* Import and export your tasks locally.

Sometime stores your tasks on your device. It needs no account and has no advertising, analytics, task telemetry, or cloud backend. The app works offline.
