Sometime keeps tasks where they belong: Today, Soon, or Sometime. Add dates and times when they help, without organizing everything into projects.

* Create tasks with natural dates and times.
* Set local reminders and recurring schedules.
* Keep different parts of life in separate Spaces.
* View tasks in Android home screen widgets and pin important tasks as notifications.
* Import and export your tasks.
* Choose light, dark, Material You, or custom colors.

Your tasks stay on your device. Sometime needs no account and has no ads, analytics, task telemetry, or cloud backend. The app works offline.
